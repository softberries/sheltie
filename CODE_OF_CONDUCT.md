# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on what is best for the
project and its community. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@softberries.dev**. All reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions and may ban
participants who violate this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
